    Common Questions

    What is no-vig fair?

    Bovada implied chance shows what a sportsbook bettor actually pays, vig included. Pinnacle raw implied chance also includes vig. Pinnacle no-vig fair divides each outcome by the sum of every outcome, including all three in a three-way market. Fairmark compares that fair with Kalshi's ask plus its estimated taker fee.

    What do under fair and derived mean?

    Under fair is the estimated discount between the fee-adjusted Kalshi price and the fair reference. Derived means a prop chance was estimated from Pinnacle's main line because the exact threshold was not quoted.

    How is the day's chance calculated?

    The public model runs 100,000 trials of the whole slate and samples each shared leg once per trial.

    Why does the demo slate refresh every 30 minutes?

    The market and combo search rebuilds the modeled slate every 30 minutes. Existing Kalshi quotes are checked on each poll, so their displayed prices can change sooner.

    Where can I search markets and combos?

    Market and combo search runs from the command line: uv run fairmark slate slate.txt --trials 100000. It prints matched markets, fair prices, estimated combo payouts, and day risk.
